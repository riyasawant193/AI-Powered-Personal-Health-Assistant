## Paracetamol (Acetaminophen) in the United States

**1. Indications:**

* **Pain relief:** Paracetamol is used to relieve mild to moderate pain, such as headaches, muscle aches, backaches, and toothaches.
* **Fever reduction:** It is also used to reduce fever.

**2. Pharmacology:**

* Paracetamol is a non-opioid analgesic and antipyretic.
* Its exact mechanism of action is not fully understood, but it is thought to reduce pain and fever by inhibiting the production of prostaglandins in the central nervous system.

**3. Dosage & Administration:**

* **Adults:** The usual dosage is 325-1000 mg every 4-6 hours, not to exceed 3000 mg per day.
* **Children:** Dosage varies depending on age and weight. Consult a healthcare professional for appropriate dosage.

**4. Interactions:**

* **Alcohol:** Combining paracetamol with alcohol can increase the risk of liver damage.
* **Warfarin:** Paracetamol can increase the risk of bleeding in people taking warfarin.
* **Other medications:** Consult a healthcare professional about potential interactions with other medications you are taking.

**5. Side Effects:**

* **Common:** Nausea, vomiting, stomach upset, diarrhea.
* **Serious:** Liver damage (rare, but serious), allergic reactions.

**6. Pregnancy & Lactation:**

* **Pregnancy:** Paracetamol is generally considered safe for use during pregnancy, but it's best to consult a healthcare professional before taking it.
* **Lactation:** Paracetamol passes into breast milk, but it is generally considered safe for breastfeeding mothers.

**7. Precautions & Warnings:**

* **Liver disease:** People with liver disease should use paracetamol with caution.
* **Kidney disease:** People with kidney disease may need to adjust the dosage.
* **Asthma:** People with asthma may have an increased risk of allergic reactions.
* **Alcohol abuse:** People who abuse alcohol should avoid paracetamol.

**8. Overdose Effects:**

* Paracetamol overdose can cause serious liver damage, even death.
* Symptoms of overdose include nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, and jaundice.
* Seek immediate medical attention if you suspect an overdose.

**9. Storage Conditions:**

* Store paracetamol at room temperature, away from moisture and heat.
* Keep it out of reach of children.

**10. Medicine Brand Names:**

* Tylenol
* Panadol
* Acetaminophen
* Many generic brands are available
